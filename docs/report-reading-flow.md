# Report reading flow

The report answers a sequence of questions: what the collaboration looks like, which prompts
support that interpretation, what could be tried next, and what the activity and accounting
can actually establish. It keeps the existing two offline views and language switch. Switching
views changes the purpose of the page; it does not generate another analysis.

## Overview: understand the person before inspecting measurements

The hero states the collaboration description, source period and analysed coverage. The first
chapter then presents the connected portrait before the featured habits. At most two selected prompts per example sit
beside the habit examples, with dates and conversation positions when provenance was recorded.
Each portrait chapter shows at most one cited prompt; its complete supporting excerpts remain
on the evidence page. The first and last selected turns provide sequence context;
these excerpts are not ranked as the strongest proof. Episode
turns can supply that provenance in older profiles. Original wording is preserved; project and
custom-name exclusions still apply.

The second chapter presents assessed abilities and measured preference shares, without
expanding their full quote collections or project radars. Those appear on the evidence page. These follow
examples rather than introducing the person as a collection of scores. A zero is an assessed
value; an unassessed ability is not a zero. An all-unassessed radar is omitted. Preference shares
retain their minimum conversation threshold and do not grade either direction as better.

Fun facts, tool usage and activity charts are background rather than the opening portrait.
Coaching stays on the factual view, separate from identity.

## Facts and evidence: verify, consider a change, then read the background

| Chapter                      | Contents                                                                                                                                                                                                          | When it appears                                                                                           |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Check the observations       | All portrait, ability and preference source excerpts, supported trait observations, dimensions with readable verified quotes, supported understanding signals, steering examples and available history comparison | Source prompts are visible and at least one component has content                                         |
| Follow the conversations     | Selected original prompts in order, dates/positions, explicit gaps and supported or unknown endings                                                                                                               | Source prompts are visible and readable episodes exist; incomplete episodes and links to them are omitted |
| Try one change               | One optional suggestion and its basis, with alternatives below it                                                                                                                                                 | Coaching is enabled, evidence is not limited and suggestions exist                                        |
| Activity and working context | Scale, rhythm, projects, available workflow measurements, instruction context, prompt habits, comparable history and optional fun facts                                                                           | Includes only available components; absent tool metadata does not create a chart of zero use              |
| Usage, coverage and method   | Recorded usage and generation accounting, sampling, evidence limits, exclusions and privacy                                                                                                                       | Always retains the method; missing usage/generation becomes a concise limitation, not an empty panel      |

Each page lists only its actual chapters, in reading order. Chapter introductions explain what
to read next without inventing a causal link or rewriting generated prose. They use neutral
interface language so the chosen first/second-person narration remains intact. Quotes remain
expanded; this change does not hide supporting or contrary evidence behind new disclosures.

## Sparse and public exports

A sparse profile keeps its limited-evidence introduction and available observations, measurements
and method. It does not fill the opening with empty featured-habit or unassessed-radar sections,
or offer coaching when the evidence is limited. Observations with no readable verified prompt
are omitted from evidence rows; the method states that limitation.

When quotes are excluded, the overview states this up front. The evidence and conversation
chapters disappear, together with their navigation links. Generated summary descriptions and
allowed aggregate measurements remain. No public export silently reveals a private prompt to
fill a chapter. Suggestion and activity introductions describe their purpose rather than promise
that omitted source evidence is visible.

Missing metadata, observed zero and unknown outcomes remain distinct. Missing usage or generation
metadata is stated under method; observed zero usage retains its measured state. An unknown
conversation outcome stays unknown even when a request sounds like completion.

## Verification and limits

Behavior tests establish chapter order across all seven looks, public/sparse conditions,
navigation targets, unchanged source quotes and recorded-date/position provenance. Render-only
changes work with compatible saved profiles and require no LLM call. No profile schema, analysis
prompt or scoring rule changes for this flow.

Before this change the factual page opened with statistics, tools and rhythm, with evidence much
further down; fun facts also interrupted the opening portrait. The new order connects story and
examples, brings evidence before background, and places optional action after the evidence.
This is a concrete reading-order improvement, not a claim that a reader study was conducted or
that all generated descriptions are free of repetition.
